# Arena Maps

All 6 maps support **Small (0.75x)**, **Medium (1.0x)**, and **Large (1.4x)** scaling and pass automated BFS spawn/pad connectivity validation (`validateMapConnectivity`).

1. **Desert Outpost** (`desert_outpost`, 180x180m Medium): Adobe walls, central fortress, speed pads, mud corners, and red explosive barrels. Best for FFA & TDM.
2. **Industrial Yard** (`industrial_yard`, 200x160m Medium): Shipping containers, overhead crane landmarks, speed ramps, and flanking lanes. Best for TDM & CTF.
3. **Frozen Lake** (`frozen_lake`, 190x190m Medium): Large central ice sheet with low traction (`iceFriction: 0.15`), snow rock cover, and pine trees. Best for LTS & FFA.
4. **Forest Maze** (`forest_maze`, 180x180m Medium): Hedge corridors, destructible wooden crates, pine trees (foliage non-blocking), and mud paths. Best for FFA & LTS.
5. **Twin Bases** (`twin_bases`, 220x150m Medium): Symmetrical Red/Blue fortresses separated by mid-bridge lanes and flanking speed pads. Built for CTF & TDM.
6. **Crater Hill** (`crater_hill`, 180x180m Medium): Glowing volcanic central caldera surrounded by crater rim openings and mud trenches. Built for KOTH & LTS.
