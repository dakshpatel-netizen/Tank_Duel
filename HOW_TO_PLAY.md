# How to Play Tank Duel Online

## Controls
- **W / S** (or Up / Down): Drive forward / reverse
- **A / D** (or Left / Right): Turn tank hull
- **Mouse**: Aim turret pin on the arena floor
- **Left Click**: Fire main cannon shell (ricochets once off walls)
- **Space / Right Click**: Use stored Power Box weapon
- **Shift**: Turbo boost (drains stamina, recharges when released)
- **Ctrl**: Handbrake sharp pivot
- **Q / E**: Scout sideways dash (Scout class only, 4s cooldown)
- **Tab**: Tap to enlarge minimap (3s), hold to view Scoreboard
- **T**: Open Quick Chat wheel (`1`–`6` to send)
- **M**: Mute / unmute audio
- **Esc**: Pause (Practice) / Match Menu (Multiplayer)

## Tank Classes
1. **Scout (80 HP)**: Fastest speed and reload (0.9s), plus Q/E sideways dash.
2. **Standard (100 HP)**: Balanced speed, 30 cannon damage, 1.1s reload.
3. **Heavy (150 HP)**: Highest cannon damage (38) and 15% frontal armor reduction within a 60° front arc.

## Power Box Items
- **Timed Buffs**: Repair Kit (+40 HP), Shield (50 HP barrier), Rapid Fire (0.4x reload), Triple Shot (3-shell spread), Speed Boost (1.4x speed + turbo), Damage Up (1.6x damage).
- **Stored Weapons**: Homing Missile, Cluster Missile (5 bomblets), Guided Missile (mouse-steered), Bouncing Shell (3 uses, 3 bounces), EMP Pulse (15m slow + weapon lock), Mines (3 stealth mines), Airstrike (3-blast strike).
